## English

**App name (30 characters):** FROHG: Frog Jump

**Subtitle / short description (App Store 30, Google Play 80 characters):**
- App Store: Hop, eat flies, reach space!
- Google Play: Hop a pixel frog from platform to platform, eat flies and climb all the way to space!

**Description:**

Ready for a jumping adventure from the swamp to outer space?

FROHG is an easy-to-learn, hard-to-put-down arcade game starring a cute pixel frog. Drag your finger to steer and hop from platform to platform to climb as high as you can.

🐸 HOW TO PLAY
• Touch and drag: the frog follows your finger.
• Land on platforms to bounce higher, and don't fall!
• Collect coins, earn EXP and level up. Every level makes your jumps stronger.

✨ FEATURES
• A sky that turns from day to night, sunset to outer space
• Past 1000 points you reach space: planets, comets and a brand-new soundtrack
• Tongue potion: drink it and snap up flies with your tongue
• Springs, moving, glass and swamp platforms
• Extra lives as you level up
• Astronaut helmet, wizard hat, barbarian helm, crown and more
• 7 frog colours, including spotted and striped patterns
• Original 8-bit music
• Plays offline, no ads, no data collection

How high can you hop?
